The problem

Study material and college news are scattered, so students miss what matters.

WhatsApp & Telegram groups

Files get buried

Lecture notes, past exams and summaries scroll away under chat messages and are hard to search.

Asking around

New students get lost

Departments, schedules, contacts, locations and procedures have no single, current guide.

Paper boards & social pages

Decisions arrive late

Exam dates, holidays and rule changes reach many students late, or not at all.

CHCollege Hub replaces all three with one trusted, searchable source.

Goals

What the first release has to achieve

  1. Let students find a study file for their course in under 30 seconds.
  2. Make college decisions reach every active user on the day they are published.
  3. Give new students a complete, up-to-date guide to the college.
  4. Build a shared library of study files that grows through student uploads.

Not in the first release

  • Grades, attendance or registration. These stay in the college's student information system.
  • Live classes, video lectures or a full learning management system.
  • Chat or social feeds between students.
  • Paid ads, paid content or a marketplace for selling notes.

Core features

Four areas, one tap apart on the bottom bar

Each area maps to a tab in the app. Use "Show in app" to open it in the preview above.

News and decisions board

  • A banner at the top of Home rotates through pinned announcements. It carries no paid ads.
  • Below it, a newest-first feed tagged Decision News Event Exam
  • Each post shows a title, body, optional image or PDF, publish date and the issuing office.
  • Urgent decisions send a push notification.

Study files

Department › Stage › Course › Type
  • Types: lecture, summary, past exam and assignment.
  • Keyword search with filters for department, stage, course, file type and semester.
  • Upload PDF, Word, PowerPoint or images. New uploads wait for moderator approval.
  • Download, save to favorites, rate and report files.

College information

  • About the college, departments and their heads, and staff contact details.
  • Academic calendar and exam schedules.
  • Campus map and building locations.
  • Common procedures and FAQs: registration, transfers and document requests.

Profile

  • Sign up with your college email, confirmed with a one-time code.
  • Department and stage personalize the files and news you see.
  • My uploads, favorites, downloads and notification settings.

  1. Step 1

    Pick course and type

    The student fills in the course and file type while uploading.

  2. Step 2

    Upload up to 25 MB

    PDF, DOCX, PPTX, JPG or PNG. Only verified students can upload.

  3. Step 3

    Scan and dedupe

    Every file is virus-scanned, and duplicates are detected.

  4. Step 4

    Moderator review

    Approve or reject from the queue, targeted at under 48 hours.

  5. Step 5

    Live in the library

    The file becomes searchable and the uploader is notified.

Who uses it

Student

Undergraduates in every year who want study files, news and a guide to the college.

Moderator

A team of 5 trusted students, covering all departments, who keep the file library clean.

College admin

The student team that reposts official news and decisions so they reach every student.

Release plan

Three phases, each unlocked by a clear gate

Phase 1 alone is enough to launch, since it covers both the news board and the file library. Every phase runs on free tools and hosting tiers.

Phase 1 · MVP · FR-1 to FR-7

Launch the core

  • Sign-up and roles
  • News board, banner and urgent alerts
  • Browse, search and upload files
  • Uploads approved by admins

Gate: pilot with 1 department

Phase 2 · Growth · FR-8 to FR-11

Open to every department

  • Moderator role and tools
  • College information pages
  • Favorites, ratings and reports
  • Personalized feed and files

Gate: all departments live

Phase 3 · Expansion · FR-12 to FR-14

Grow beyond one college

  • Offline saved files
  • Comments and reactions
  • Multiple colleges

Gate: ongoing, driven by feedback

Risks and decisions

What could go wrong, and the plan for it

Copyrighted textbooks get uploaded

Moderation queue, a report button, clear upload rules and fast takedown.

Too few files at launch, so students don't return

Moderators and class representatives seed the library before launch.

Official news is missed because the college is not a partner

Student admins repost each decision with the original document attached. Posting takes under 2 minutes.

Fake or unofficial "decisions" spread

Only verified admin accounts can post, and every post shows the issuing office.

Storage costs grow with uploads

Free hosting tiers, a 25 MB file limit, duplicate detection and compression.
